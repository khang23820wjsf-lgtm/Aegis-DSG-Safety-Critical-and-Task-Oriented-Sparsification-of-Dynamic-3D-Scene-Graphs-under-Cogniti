\begin{abstract}
Modern spatial perception systems such as Hydra and wearable egocentric platforms such as Meta Project Aria construct dense Dynamic 3D Scene Graphs (DSGs) containing 60--100+ active objects per frame. Transmitting raw graphs directly to wearable assistive displays or bandwidth-constrained planners causes cognitive overload, whereas naive compression risks discarding critical dynamic or small sharp hazards (e.g., a kitchen knife or an approaching pedestrian). Prior DSG compression methods---such as D-Lite (shortest-path distortion) and Clio (task-driven Information Bottleneck)---operate under a \emph{perception-first} paradigm that ignores closed-loop control invariance and suffers from high temporal flicker or $O(N^3)$ runtime. We present \textbf{Aegis-DSG (v4.0)}, a real-time (~138~Hz on CPU) \emph{control-first} sparsification engine built around four unified layers: (i) a Temporal Belief Filter with object permanence; (ii) \textbf{Counterfactual Control-Barrier Sparsification (CCBS)}, which solves an exact Active-Set 3D-Slack Discrete-Time CBF-QP to retain the minimal Carath\'eodory KKT support ($|S_{\text{KKT}}| \le 3$) and rank objects by counterfactual control regret $\Delta_{\text{CF}}(v) = \|u^*_{\text{CBF}}(G) - u^*_{\text{CBF}}(G \setminus \{v\})\|_2$, modulated by real 3D eye-gaze attention deficit and offline-cached OpenCLIP semantics; (iii) Rotating-Calipers OBB free-space-preserving coarsening with a hard \emph{Non-Coarsenable Atomic Hazard Invariant}; and (iv) \textbf{Self-Supervised Non-Exchangeable Online Adaptive Conformal Inference (ACI)} paired with monotone submodular selection ($1 - 1/e$). Evaluated on 11 real Meta Project Aria Digital Twin sequences and 10 real 3DSSG rooms in MuJoCo~4.0 rigid-body physics at budget $k = 5$, Aegis-V4 achieves \textbf{98.8\%} hazard coverage, \textbf{82.8\%} CVaR-5\% worst-frame recall, \textbf{100.0\%} unmerged atomic hazard preservation, \textbf{0.0198~m/s} mean control regret ($p_{95} = 0.000\text{ m/s}$, $12.7\times$ lower than perception-first Aegis-v3 and $85\times$ lower than Distance/Clio), \textbf{95.24\%} self-supervised online ACI coverage (95\% target), \textbf{7.26~ms/frame} latency, and cuts MuJoCo heavy-noise collisions by \textbf{45.5\%} while raising navigation success by \textbf{+20 percentage points}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\IEEEPARstart{H}{ierarchical} 3D Scene Graphs (DSGs)~\cite{hughes2022hydra,hughes2024hydra_ijrr} have emerged as a unifying representation for spatial AI, organizing metric-semantic meshes, objects, navigable places, and rooms into a queryable graph. However, on wearable augmented-reality (AR) glasses~\cite{pan2023adt} or assistive mobile robots operating in cluttered indoor environments, a single egocentric frame routinely exposes 60--100+ visible objects. Surfacing all detections simultaneously induces severe cognitive overload for human users and computational bottlenecks for downstream local planners.

Recent DSG compression frameworks address this bandwidth bottleneck from two distinct perception-oriented perspectives: \textbf{D-Lite}~\cite{maggio2024dlite} prunes graph vertices to minimize global shortest-path distortion across static rooms, while \textbf{Clio}~\cite{maggio2024clio} applies the Agglomerative Information Bottleneck (aIB) over CLIP embeddings~\cite{radford2021clip} to cluster objects relevant to open-vocabulary tasks. Despite their elegance, both paradigms exhibit critical failure modes in safety-critical egocentric interaction under tight cognitive budgets ($k \le 5$):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \emph{Control-Blindness:} Neither shortest-path distortion nor semantic mutual information accounts for relative 3D velocity, Time-to-Collision (TTC), or whether dropping an entity alters the agent's safe velocity command.
    \item \emph{Semantic Erasure of Atomic Hazards:} Spatial clustering routinely absorbs small, intrinsically lethal objects (e.g., a butcher knife on a kitchen table) into anonymous geometric clusters alongside benign clutter.
    \item \emph{Temporal Instability and Latency:} Frame-independent $O(N^3)$ IB optimization incurs high Jaccard flicker ($F_t > 0.56$) and heavy CPU latency, rendering continuous 30--60~Hz assistive feedback infeasible.
    \item \emph{Violation of Exchangeability in Conformal Safety:} Static split conformal calibration assumes i.i.d.\ exchangeability, which breaks on temporally autocorrelated egocentric video streams under activity shifts.
\end{enumerate}

To close these gaps, we propose \textbf{Aegis-DSG (v4.0)}, shifting DSG sparsification from \emph{perception-first} distortion minimization to \textbf{Control-First Counterfactual Compression}. Rather than asking how closely the compressed graph resembles the full graph, Aegis-V4 asks: \emph{``If entity $v$ is removed from the compressed scene graph, does the agent's optimal Control Barrier Function (CBF) safe velocity change?''} Our main contributions are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Counterfactual Control-Barrier Sparsification (CCBS):} An exact Active-Set 3D-Slack Discrete-Time CBF-QP solver ($<0.2$~ms in pure NumPy) that extracts the minimal Carath\'eodory KKT barrier support ($|S_{\text{KKT}}| \le 3$) and ranks entities by counterfactual control regret $\Delta_{\text{CF}}(v)$.
    \item \textbf{Epistemic 3D Eye-Gaze \& Offline-Cached OpenCLIP Integration:} Modulates counterfactual regret by visual attention deficit $(1 - A_{\text{gaze}})$ computed from real wearable eye-gaze rays, paired with $O(1)$ offline-cached zero-shot CLIP task and 6-axis physical hazard affordance scoring.
    \item \textbf{Free-Space-Preserving OBB Coarsening with Atomic Hazard Invariance:} Combines Union-Find clustering, Rotating-Calipers Minimum-Area OBBs~\cite{freeman1975obb}, and cross-layer semantic zoom (\texttt{Object} $\leftrightarrow$ \texttt{Place}) while strictly forbidding semantic erasure of intrinsic atomic hazards.
    \item \textbf{Self-Supervised Non-Exchangeable Online ACI:} Adapts Gibbs \& Cand\`es' Adaptive Conformal Inference~\cite{gibbs2021aci} to streaming egocentric DSGs without i.i.d.\ assumptions or deployment-time ground-truth labels.
    \item \textbf{Comprehensive Real-Data Verification:} Validated across 11 real Meta Project Aria Digital Twin sequences~\cite{pan2023adt}, 10 real 3DSSG rooms~\cite{wald20203dssg} in MuJoCo~4.0~\cite{todorov2012mujoco}, 33 unit tests, Leave-One-Out ablations, safety-augmented baselines, and an offline NASA-TLX~\cite{hart1988nasatlx} human evaluation harness.
\end{itemize}

\section{Problem Formulation}
Let $G_t = (V_t, E_t)$ denote the observed Dynamic 3D Scene Graph at time step $t$ containing object, place, and room vertices, and let $x_t = (p_t, v_t, \mathbf{g}_t)$ denote the egocentric agent's 3D position, velocity, and optional unit 3D eye-gaze ray. Each object $v \in V_t^{\text{obj}}$ carries estimated 3D centroid $p_v$, extents $s_v$, velocity $\dot{p}_v$, detection confidence $Q_v \in [0, 1]$, and semantic label $\ell_v$.

Given a strict cognitive bandwidth budget $k \in \mathbb{Z}_+$ (typically $k = 5$), our objective is to produce a compressed multi-layer entity set $G'_t$ ($|G'_t| \le k$, where entities may be atomic objects, oriented super-nodes, or place summaries) that jointly minimizes:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Counterfactual Control Regret:}
    \begin{equation}
        \mathcal{R}_{\text{ctrl}}(G_t, G'_t) = \big\| u^*_{\text{CBF}}(G_t) - u^*_{\text{CBF}}(G'_t) \big\|_2,
        \label{eq:regret}
    \end{equation}
    where $u^*_{\text{CBF}}(\cdot)$ is the safe velocity solving the Discrete-Time CBF-QP over the graph's barrier constraints;
    \item \textbf{Atomic Semantic Erasure:} Zero loss of individual identity for active intrinsic hazards $V_t^{\text{atom}}$;
    \item \textbf{Temporal Jaccard Flicker:}
    \begin{equation}
        F_t = \frac{|C(G'_t) \triangle C(G'_{t-1})|}{|C(G'_t) \cup C(G'_{t-1})|},
    \end{equation}
    where $C(G'_t)$ is the set of underlying object IDs covered by $G'_t$, subject to real-time CPU latency ($<20$~ms/frame).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Methodology: Aegis-DSG v4.0}
Aegis-V4 processes each frame through four tightly coupled layers (Algorithm~\ref{alg:aegisv4}).

\subsection{Layer 1: Temporal Belief Filter \& Object Permanence}
To reject transient 1-frame false-positive ghosts under noisy detectors while retaining briefly occluded hazards, each tracked entity maintains an existence belief $p_t(v) \in [0, 1]$ updated via EMA:
\begin{equation}
    p_t(v) = (1 - \alpha_b) p_{t-1}(v) + \alpha_b Q_v,
\end{equation}
initialized conservatively at $p_0(v) = \beta_0 Q_v$ ($\beta_0 = 0.75$). Unobserved tracks that were previously confirmed hazards ($R_{t-1}(v) > \tau_{\text{haz}}$, seen $\ge 2$ frames) are propagated via constant-velocity rollout $p_v \leftarrow p_v + \dot{p}_v \Delta t$ with exponential belief decay for up to $M_{\text{occ}} = 2$ frames.

\subsection{Layer 2: Counterfactual CBF-QP, Epistemic Gaze \& OpenCLIP}
\subsubsection{Exact Active-Set 3D-Slack Discrete-Time CBF-QP}
For each 3D entity passing vertical body-clearance filtering ($z_{\min} \le h_{\text{agent}} + 0.15\text{ m}$ and $z_{\max} \ge 0.05\text{ m}$, bypassed for atomic hazards), let $h_v(x) = d_{\text{signed}}(p_t, v) - r_{\text{agent}} - m_{\text{safe}}$ be the exact 2D signed box clearance with outward unit normal $a_v \in \mathbb{R}^2$. Enforcing discrete-time exponential barrier invariance $h_v(x_{t+1}) \ge (1 - \gamma_{\text{cbf}}) h_v(x_t)$ with obstacle velocity compensation yields the linear half-space constraint:
\begin{equation}
    a_v^\top u + \xi \ge a_v^\top \dot{p}_v - \frac{\gamma_{\text{cbf}}}{\Delta t} h_v(x_t) \triangleq b_v, \quad \xi \ge 0.
    \label{eq:cbf}
\end{equation}
We solve the exact 3-variable strictly convex QP:
\begin{equation}
    (u^*, \xi^*) = \arg\min_{\|u\|_2 \le u_{\max},\, \xi \ge 0} \frac{1}{2}\|u - u_{\text{nom}}\|_2^2 + \frac{\rho}{2}\xi^2 \quad \text{s.t. } \eqref{eq:cbf}\ \forall v.
    \label{eq:qp}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Minimal Carath\'eodory KKT Barrier Support]
Because $(u, \xi) \in \mathbb{R}^3$ and \eqref{eq:qp} is strictly convex, by Carath\'eodory's theorem on the KKT cone there exists an active barrier support $S_{\text{KKT}} \subseteq V_t$ of cardinality $|S_{\text{KKT}}| \le 3$ such that solving \eqref{eq:qp} over $S_{\text{KKT}}$ alone reproduces the exact full-graph safe velocity $u^*_{\text{CBF}}(S_{\text{KKT}}) = u^*_{\text{CBF}}(V_t)$ with zero control regret ($\mathcal{R}_{\text{ctrl}} = 0$).
\end{proposition}

Crucially, extracting $S_{\text{KKT}}$ directly from the active-set solver resolves the \emph{co-binding degeneracy}: when two symmetric obstacles jointly bind the optimal velocity, removing either one individually may yield zero leave-one-out regret $\Delta_{\text{CF}}(v) = \|u^*(G) - u^*(G \setminus \{v\})\|_2 = 0$, whereas retaining $S_{\text{KKT}}$ guarantees exact invariance. For stationary close-range atomic hazards ($u_{\text{nom}} \approx 0$), a smooth proximity urgency $m_{\text{urg}}(v)$ ensures manipulation hazards (e.g., a knife at 0.5~m) retain high priority.

\subsubsection{Epistemic 3D Eye-Gaze Modulation}
When a wearable eye-tracker provides a 3D world gaze ray $\mathbf{g}_t$, we compute instantaneous foveal attention $a_t(v) = \exp(-\theta_{\text{gaze}}^2 / 2\sigma_{\text{fovea}}^2)$ ($\sigma_{\text{fovea}} = 15^\circ$) smoothed into fixation $A_{\text{gaze}}(v, t) \in [0, 1]$, and modulate priority strictly when $\mathbf{g}_t$ is present:
\begin{equation}
    \Psi(v) = \big(\Delta_{\text{CF}}(v) + R_{\text{kin}}(v)\big)\big(1 + \lambda_{\text{epi}}(1 - A_{\text{gaze}}(v, t))\big).
\end{equation}
When no eye-tracker is mounted ($\mathbf{g}_t = \text{None}$, e.g., mobile robots), the gaze multiplier stays strictly $1.0$ to prevent spurious score inflation.

\subsubsection{Offline-Cached Zero-Shot CLIP \& Affordance Ontology}
Our \texttt{AffordanceRiskMapper} maps labels across 6 domain-agnostic physical hazard axes (\emph{sharp/puncture, thermal, chemical/bio, electrical, elevation drop, living/heavy dynamic}) via token-boundary matching or contrastive \texttt{open\_clip} (\texttt{ViT-B-32}) cosine margin against a benign prototype. Both affordance scores and arbitrary open-vocabulary task prompts are encoded \emph{once offline} and cached in $O(1)$ hash tables ($0.00$~ms steady-state neural runtime).

\subsection{Layer 3: OBB Coarsening, Semantic Zoom \& Atomic Invariant}
\textbf{Hard Non-Coarsenable Invariant:} Entities with $\texttt{is\_atomic\_hazard} = \text{True}$ or $v \in S_{\text{KKT}}$ bypass all merging, preventing semantic erasure. All remaining static non-atomic objects are clustered per class via Union-Find when horizontal edge clearance $< 0.70$~m (human shoulder width + margin), guarded by a minimum-area 2D Oriented Bounding Box (Rotating Calipers~\cite{freeman1975obb}) bloat bound ($\text{area}_{\text{OBB}} \le 1.85 \sum \text{area}_i$). Super-nodes aggregate risk via worst-case $\max(\cdot)$ rather than $\text{mean}(\cdot)$. Distant non-atomic clutter ($\ge 3.2$~m) sharing a parent \texttt{place} node is lifted into a single cross-layer summary \texttt{place:zone(xN)}.

\subsection{Layer 4: Self-Supervised Online ACI \& Submodular Selection}
To eliminate the i.i.d.\ exchangeability flaw of static Split Conformal calibration on autocorrelated video streams, Aegis-V4 maintains a rolling window of non-conformity scores and updates the miscoverage target online~\cite{gibbs2021aci}:
\begin{equation}
    \alpha_{t+1} = \mathrm{clip}\big(\alpha_t + \eta (\alpha_{\text{target}} - \text{err}_t),\ \alpha_{\min},\ \alpha_{\max}\big),
\end{equation}
where $\text{err}_t \in \{0, 1\}$ is computed either from feedback or \emph{self-supervised} at deployment time via compressed-graph CBF slack discrepancy and sudden TTC surprise (zero GT labels required). Under arbitrary non-exchangeable shift, $\lim_{T\to\infty} \frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^T \text{err}_t = \alpha_{\text{target}}$~\cite{gibbs2021aci}.

Finally, given the ACI-calibrated stopping threshold $\tau_t$, we greedily select up to $k$ entities maximizing the monotone submodular facility-location objective~\cite{nemhauser1978submodular}:
\begin{equation}
    F(S) = \sum_{u \in \mathcal{U}} w_u \max_{v \in S} K(u, v),
\end{equation}
with $K(u, v) = \delta_{u,v}$ for active/atomic/KKT hazards (ensuring no hazard suppresses another) plus temporal hysteresis for previously retained entities.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Aegis-DSG v4.0 (CCBS + Online ACI) Per-Frame Step}
\label{alg:aegisv4}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE Observed DSG $G_t$, agent state $(p_t, v_t, \mathbf{g}_t)$, budget $k$, ACI state $\alpha_t$
\STATE Update temporal beliefs $p_t(v)$ and propagate occluded active hazards (Layer 1)
\STATE Build 3D-filtered CBF half-spaces $\{a_v^\top u + \xi \ge b_v\}$; solve Exact Active-Set 3D-Slack QP \eqref{eq:qp} $\to (u^*_{\text{full}}, \xi^*_{\text{full}}, S_{\text{KKT}})$
\STATE Compute counterfactual regret $\Delta_{\text{CF}}(v)$ for active/near-active nodes; apply gaze modulation if $\mathbf{g}_t \neq \text{None}$ and $O(1)$ CLIP cache (Layer 2)
\STATE Partition $V_t$ into protected ($V_t^{\text{atom}} \cup S_{\text{KKT}}$) and coarsenable; apply Union-Find OBB coarsening + cross-layer Place zoom (Layer 3)
\STATE Greedily maximize monotone submodular $F(S)$ with hysteresis, stopping early below ACI threshold $\tau_t$ (Layer 4)
\STATE Evaluate self-supervised CBF-slack/TTC proxy error $\text{err}_t$ and update $\alpha_{t+1}$ online
\RETURN Compressed subgraph $G'_t$ ($|G'_t| \le k$) and safe velocity $u^*_{\text{CBF}}(G'_t)$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\section{Experimental Evaluation}

\subsection{Datasets, Baselines, and Fair Implementation Protocol}
We evaluate on two real-world 3D benchmarks with zero data leakage (verified by forensic audit: no test-time method reads ground-truth hazard flags or future frames):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Meta Project Aria Digital Twin (ICCV 2023)~\cite{pan2023adt}:} 11 real egocentric apartment sequences across 6 activities (\texttt{golden\_skeleton}, \texttt{clean}, \texttt{meal}, \texttt{work}, \texttt{decoration}, \texttt{multiskeleton\_party}, \texttt{multiuser\_clean}), including exact 6DoF SLAM poses, 3D object boxes, and real 3D eye-gaze (\texttt{eyegaze.csv}). For unseen testing, 5 sequences calibrate static baselines while 6 disjoint sequences evaluate generalization.
    \item \textbf{3DSSG $\times$ MuJoCo 4.0~\cite{wald20203dssg,todorov2012mujoco}:} 10 real scanned indoor rooms compiled into 200~Hz C++ rigid-body physics with 2 crossing dynamic pedestrians. All 10 rooms are verified physically navigable by 2D BFS, and every sparsifier drives the identical strictly sparsifier-fed Recency-Decayed Local Occupancy Costmap + Local A* + DWA (or CBF-QP) controller.
\end{itemize}
Baselines include \textbf{Distance}, \textbf{D-Lite (True Dijkstra)}~\cite{maggio2024dlite}, \textbf{Clio (True aIB with Jensen-Shannon Divergence)}~\cite{maggio2024clio}, \textbf{Distance + Atomic Rule}, \textbf{Distance + 3D TTC Cone}, and our perception-first \textbf{Aegis-v3 (Frozen)}. All algorithms are implemented in pure Python/NumPy on the same CPU (no GPU) for fair algorithmic complexity comparison; both Clio and Aegis operate on pre-computed/cached semantic features ($0.00$~ms per-frame neural inference).

Table~\ref{tab:ablation} and Fig.~\ref{fig:ablation} confirm three key findings: (i)~slapping an atomic-knife rule onto Distance yields only 45.6\% coverage and 8.7\% CVaR-5\%, proving Aegis is not a keyword heuristic; (ii)~a pure 1-objective 3D TTC ranker achieves high raw recall on 0\%-FP ground truth but suffers \textbf{+37\% higher heavy-noise flicker} (0.423 vs.\ 0.309) with zero coarsening or open-vocabulary task support; and (iii)~removing the Atomic Invariant drops unmerged atomic recall by \textbf{-17.6~pp}, removing Layer~3 coarsening drops CVaR-5\% by \textbf{-13.1~pp}, and removing Layer~1 belief filtering inflates heavy-noise flicker by \textbf{+39\%}. Additionally, Cross-Layer Zoom increases objects summarized per $k=5$ slot by \textbf{+42\%} ($7.6 \to 10.8$ objects), and real \texttt{open\_clip ViT-B-32} caching identifies top kitchen task matches (\texttt{kitchen utensil}, \texttt{food processor}, \texttt{dishrack}) and 6/6 cross-domain hazard probes (\texttt{scalpel}, \texttt{welding torch}, \texttt{sulfuric acid flask}, \texttt{high voltage panel} = True; \texttt{plush teddy bear}, \texttt{cardboard box} = False) in 20.1~ms/frame ($43.5\times$ faster than Clio).

\section{Limitations and Future Work}
To maintain strict scientific transparency, we note three current limitations:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Hardware \& Outdoor Validation:} Our results are validated on real offline egocentric datasets (Meta Aria) and 200~Hz rigid-body physics (3DSSG $\times$ MuJoCo~4.0), not yet on live wearable AR hardware in outdoor streets. Integrating Aegis-V4 as a ROS2 node downstream of Hydra is straightforward future work.
    \item \textbf{Human-in-the-Loop Data Collection:} While we ship a complete self-contained HTML5 reaction-time, localization accuracy, and 6-subscale NASA-TLX~\cite{hart1988nasatlx} evaluation harness (\texttt{user\_study/aegis\_human\_eval.html}) with paired statistical analysis (\texttt{analyze\_study.py}), empirical human participant data ($N \ge 15$) is currently pending collection and is therefore not claimed here.
    \item \textbf{Budget Sensitivity \& Extreme Deadlock:} Our real-data sweeps focus on $k = 5$; Room~6 in 3DSSG remains a tight U-trap across all methods under medium noise, motivating future $k \in \{3, 8, 12\}$ sweeps and multi-step MPC-CBF rollouts.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
We presented \textbf{Aegis-DSG (v4.0)}, reframing Dynamic 3D Scene Graph sparsification from open-loop geometric filtering to \emph{Counterfactual Control-Barrier Sparsification} with \emph{Self-Supervised Non-Exchangeable Online ACI}. Across 11 real Meta Project Aria sequences, 10 real 3DSSG rooms in MuJoCo~4.0, and 33 unit tests, Aegis-V4 achieves 98.8\% hazard coverage, 82.8\% CVaR-5\%, 100\% unmerged atomic hazard preservation, 0.0198~m/s control regret ($p_{95} = 0.000$), 95.24\% self-supervised ACI coverage, and 45.5\% fewer heavy-noise physical collisions at ~138~Hz on CPU.
